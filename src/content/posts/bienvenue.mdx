For the past few years, I've been giving public sessions, speaking at conferences, appearing on podcasts, and writing about creative AI, its real uses, its limits, and its tensions with cinema, storytelling, and authorship.

Each time, the same thing happens. Legitimate questions. Productive disagreements. And sometimes, noise.

This blog is where I think out loud. Not to build a personal brand, but to have a place to point people to when they want to read more, instead of responding to every comment separately.

## What you'll find here

- Notes on my experiences with generative tools in image, video, and text
- Reflections on AI and collective creation
- Commentary on current events through the lens of AI and creativity
- Observations on how AI and creativity are unfolding in the news and public conversation
- Announcements for my courses at Concordia and my bimonthly events
- Questions people have asked me, and my answers

## What you won't find here

Certainties.

I don't know where this is going. I'm taking notes.
