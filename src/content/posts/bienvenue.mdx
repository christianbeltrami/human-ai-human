For the past few years, I've been giving public sessions on creative AI — its real uses, its limits, its tensions with cinema, storytelling, and authorship.

Every time, there are legitimate questions, productive disagreements, and sometimes… noise.

This blog is the space where I think out loud. Not to build a personal brand, but to have something to point to when people ask *where to read more* rather than responding to each comment separately.

## What you'll find here

- Notes on my experiences with generative tools (image, video, text)
- Reflections on AI and collective creation
- Announcements for my courses at Concordia and my bimonthly events
- Questions people have asked me — and my answers

## What you won't find here

Certainties. I don't know where this is going. I'm taking notes.
