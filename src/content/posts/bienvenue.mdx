If you're curious about what happens when AI meets creative work, you've probably run into some version of the same questions: Is AI going to replace what I do? Should I be using it? Where's the line between tool and authorship?

For the past few years, I've been speaking, writing, and giving public sessions on creative AI, its real uses, its limits, and its tensions with cinema, storytelling, and authorship. Every time, the same thing happens. Productive disagreements. Good questions. And sometimes, noise.

This blog is where I think out loud about what actually happens when generative AI enters creative work. It's also a place to point people to when they want to read more, instead of responding to every comment separately.

I'll be writing not just about AI discourse, but about methods, experiments, failures, and what actually holds up in professional creative workflows.

## What you'll find here

- Notes from my experience with generative tools across image, video, and text
- Observations on how AI and creativity are unfolding in the news and public conversation
- Announcements for my courses at Concordia and my bimonthly events
- Questions people have asked me, and my answers

## What you won't find here

Certainties.

I don't know exactly where this is going. I'm taking notes.
