**Time context:** Late May 2026  
**Audience:** Students, creators, researchers, and technical teams who want to understand where Seedance is publicly exposed.

> **Independent resource notice**
>
> This catalogue is an independent Human-AI-Human educational resource. It is not a recommendation, a ranking, or an endorsement of any platform. It documents the public landscape of Seedance access points for literacy and editorial verification purposes.

This page lists platforms that have **a model page, generator, playground, API documentation, workflow, or product integration** built around Seedance. Official access points, verified platforms, aggregators, and independent sites still under review are classified separately.

> **Read first:** model availability, pricing, quotas, watermarks, and terms of use change rapidly. Every entry should be re-verified before being relied on for production.

---

## Seedance model index

| Family | Summary |
|---|---|
| **Seedance 1.0 / 1.0 Lite / 1.0 Pro / 1.0 Pro Fast** | 1.x variants generally exposed as text-to-video and image-to-video, often via API or playground. |
| **Seedance 1.5 Pro** | More advanced version, often positioned around video generation with audio or higher-quality video results. |
| **Seedance 2.0 / 2.0 Fast** | Next-generation multimodal version with text, image, video, and audio inputs, multi-reference support, and native audio depending on platform. |

## Recommended primary choices by context

These choices are flagged as **entry points to validate** for their respective category, not as universal recommendations.

- **Official access / enterprise API:** BytePlus ModelArk / VolcEngine Ark.
- **Battle-tested developer APIs:** Replicate, fal.ai, Vercel AI Gateway, Segmind, Magic Hour, Kie.ai, WaveSpeedAI.
- **No-code creative:** CapCut / Dreamina, Runway, Higgsfield, Artlist, getimg.ai, OpenArt, Media.io, Topview AI.
- **Technical workflows:** ComfyUI / Comfy Cloud, RunComfy.

## Pages that should not be classified as direct Seedance access

These pages may be useful for comparison or landscape literacy, but they do not constitute direct access to Seedance.

| **"Seedance-like", "alternative to Seedance", "matches Seedance" pages** | Useful for comparing video models, but they do not prove generation runs on Seedance. |
| **Standalone social mentions (LinkedIn, forums, marketing posts without a product page / API)** | A social mention alone is not enough: there must be at minimum a product page, model ID, playground, API documentation, or terms of use. |

## Methodology

This catalogue prioritizes **factual classification over recommendation**. Platforms are included only if they have a model page, generator, playground, API documentation, workflow, or product integration built around Seedance.

Official access points, verified platforms, aggregators, and independent sites still under review are classified separately. Source URLs are listed at the entry level for audit or editorial verification.

Because availability, pricing, and terms of use change frequently, every entry should be re-verified before being relied on for production or a client deliverable.
